Mayor Alex L. Advincula

Insights and Updates from the City of Imus

Leadership and Vision

Alex Lacson Advincula, popularly known as “AA,” represents transparency and accountable governance. Born on December 28, 1969 in Tanzang Luma, Imus Cavite, AA has dedicated his life to public service.

He completed his education locally and nationally, earning degrees in Criminology and Entrepreneurial Management. AA has served as Municipal Councilor, Board Member, and Representative of the Third District of Cavite, bringing progressive programs such as the “Ospital ng Imus”, the City Grandstand, and a modern Land Transportation Office.

Elected Mayor in 2022 and re-elected in 2025, AA expanded his agenda from 5 to 8 points, reflecting the trust and confidence of Imuseños. His vision is to deliver genuine, lasting change for a better Imus.
