City Public Library

The City Public Library of Imus is a public library that provides access to a wide range of books, periodicals, and other resources.

It aims to promote literacy and lifelong learning among the residents of Imus.

For more information, visit the official website of the City Government of Imus.

Assistance

The City Government of Imus provides various assistance programs to help residents in need.

These programs include financial assistance, medical assistance, and educational assistance.

For more information, visit the official website of the City Government of Imus.

Citizen's Charter

The Citizen's Charter is a document that outlines the services provided by the City Government of Imus, including the requirements, procedures, and processing times for each service.

It aims to promote transparency and accountability in government services.

For more information, visit the official website of the City Government of Imus.

EBOSS

The Electronic Business One Stop Shop (eBOSS) is an online platform that allows business owners to register their businesses, pay taxes, and obtain permits and licenses from the City Government of Imus.

It aims to streamline the process of doing business in the city and make it more convenient for entrepreneurs.

For more information, visit the official website of the City Government of Imus.
