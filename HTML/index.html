City of Imus

Imus, officially the City of Imus, is a 3rd class component city in the province of Cavite, Philippines. According to the 2020 census, it has a population of 403,785 people.

Imus was the site of two major Katipunan battles during the Philippine Revolution. The Battle of Imus was fought in 1896 and the Battle of Alapan, in 1898. The Alapan site is also the location of the Philippine flag's first unfurling, an act that symbolized the country's declaration of independence from Spain.
